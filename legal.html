Legal Notice & Terms of Service

1. What RadarJobs is

RadarJobs is a job discovery and aggregation service. We collect publicly available remote job postings from company career pages and public job-board APIs, normalise them, and let you search and filter them.

We are not a recruitment agency, we are not an employer, and we do not participate in any hiring decision. Every listing links to the original posting on the company's own site — applications happen there, not here.

2. Accuracy of listings

Job data comes from third parties and can be outdated, duplicated or removed without notice. We flag postings older than 30 days as "ghost" and drop anything older than 90 days, but we cannot guarantee that a listing is still open. Always confirm on the original posting before applying.

Salary ranges are shown only when the source publishes them. When we show "Salary not disclosed", the employer did not publish a range — we do not estimate or invent figures.
